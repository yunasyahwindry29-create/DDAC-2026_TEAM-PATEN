## 1. Overview

**DJPb Data Analytics Competition (DDAC) 2026**
Theme: *"Empowering Treasury Through Data Stories and AI Innovations."*

Organized under: Kementerian Keuangan / DJPb — Treasury BigData × TreasurAI.

### Competition classes (Kelas Perlombaan)

There are **2 competition categories**. A team may register in either:

| Category | Output |
|---|---|
| **Data Story Telling** | Data visualization based on data storytelling (*Visualisasi data berbasis data storytelling*) |
| **AInnovation** | TreasurAI use case within the scope of DJPb / Kemenkeu (*Use Case TreasurAI lingkup DJPb/Kemenkeu*) |

> This reference targets **Data Story Telling**. AInnovation rules are summarized at the end for context.

---

## 2. General Rules (Ketentuan Umum)

- Each team may register in **2 (two) competition categories** in DDAC 2026 (Data Story Telling and AInnovation).
- All participating teams are divided into **6 regions**, following the *Regional Chief Economist* (RCE) regional split:
  1. **Bali dan Nusa Tenggara**
  2. **Jawa**
  3. **Kalimantan**
  4. **Maluku dan Papua**
  5. **Sulawesi**
  6. **Sumatera**
- For the advanced stage, the selection of **finalist teams** (*tim finalis*) will take into account representation from each region.

## 3. Data Story Telling — Rules (Peraturan)

### Eligibility & registration

- Participants register and upload the following via the **DDAC 2026 site**:
  - **Surat Tugas** (assignment letter) from their respective work unit
  - **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**
  - **Surat Pernyataan Orisinalitas** (statement of originality)
- Participants must be **ASN (civil servants) within DJPb** and **not part of the competition committee** (*panitia*).
- Teams consist of a **maximum of 3 people**.
- All members of a single team must come from the **same Eselon II unit** at the time of registration.

### Required submission (Submisi)

Participants submit their entry in the following forms:

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Short use case description (*Deskripsi singkat use case*) | **Mandatory (wajib)** |
| Link to a **dashboard or web app** | **Mandatory (wajib)** |
| Presentation (PowerPoint / Tableau Public / other) | **Mandatory (wajib)** |
| Short video (if a dashboard/web app is built) | **Optional (opsional)** |

- **Dataset Catalog** (*Katalog Dataset*) is accessible on the DDAC site.

### Dataset usage

- The provided dataset **must be used** by participants and **may be combined** with **other datasets**
  sourced from **independent surveys** (*survei mandiri*) or **other institutions** (*instansi lain*).
  The validity/authenticity of any added data **must be proven** (*wajib dibuktikan keabsahannya*).
- **Disclaimer:** data obtained from the committee may **only be used for this competition** and **must not be redistributed** (*tidak disebarluaskan*).

### Selection & process flow

1. **Submissions** are reviewed by the panel of judges (*dewan juri*) and confirmed by the committee, selecting teams as **advanced participants** (*peserta lanjutan*).
2. Selected **Data Story Telling teams** undergo a **bootcamp and training**.
3. Bootcamp results are **presented before the panel of judges** (*Dewan Juri*).
4. **3 (three) winners** (*juara*) are selected and announced by the committee — the decision is **final and cannot be contested** (*tidak dapat diganggu gugat*).
5. The results (idea, analysis method, and visualization) **may be adopted and used by DJPb** for project development at DJPb.
6. Detailed competition information is available on the **DDAC 2026 site**.

## 4. Judging Criteria (Kriteria Penilaian) — Data Story Telling

| # | Main criterion | Sub-criteria |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Data Quality** (*Kualitas Data*) | • Amount and type of data (*Jumlah dan jenis data*)<br>• Data processing technique (*Teknik pengolahan data*) |
| 2 | **Visualization Quality** (*Kualitas Visualisasi*) | • Type and composition of information (*Jenis dan komposisi informasi*)<br>• Visualization technique (*Teknik visualisasi*)<br>• Storytelling technique (*Teknik story telling*) |
| 3 | **Analysis Output** (*Output Hasil analisis*) | • Type of output produced (*Jenis output yang dihasilkan*)<br>• Presentation material (*Bahan Presentasi*) |

## 5. Practical Checklist (derived from rules)

Use this as a pre-submission checklist for the Data Story Telling track:

- [ ] Team of **max 3 people**, all from the **same Eselon II unit**.
- [ ] All members are **ASN within DJPb** and **not committee members**.
- [ ] **Surat Tugas** uploaded.
- [ ] **NDA** uploaded.
- [ ] **Surat Pernyataan Orisinalitas** uploaded.
- [ ] **Short use case description** prepared *(mandatory)*.
- [ ] **Live link** to a dashboard or web app *(mandatory)*.
- [ ] **Presentation** (PowerPoint / Tableau Public / other) *(mandatory)*.
- [ ] **Short demo video** if a dashboard/web app is built *(optional, recommended)*.
- [ ] Provided **dataset catalog data is used**; any **additional data has proven authenticity**.
- [ ] Confirm committee data is **used only for this competition** and **not redistributed**.

### Design notes for scoring well
- Lead with a clear **narrative arc** (problem → key insights → public impact) — the criteria explicitly reward *storytelling technique*, not just charts.
- Make **data processing** visible/defensible (show transformations, cleaning, joins) for the *Data Quality* criterion.
- Choose **chart types deliberately** ("type and composition of information") and keep visual design clean for *Visualization Quality*.
- Tie the **output** back to a concrete treasury/DJPb use case — outputs may be adopted by DJPb, so framing for reuse helps.

## 6. AInnovation — Summary (for context, not the target track)

Eligibility/registration rules are **the same** as Data Story Telling (ASN within DJPb, max 3 per team, same Eselon II unit; upload Surat Tugas, NDA, and Surat Pernyataan Orisinalitas).

**Required submission:**
- Short use case description — **mandatory**
- Link to the **application or innovation document** — **mandatory**
- Presentation (PowerPoint / other) — **mandatory**
- Short video if an application is built — **optional**

**Judging criteria:**
| Main criterion | Sub-criteria |
|---|---|
| **Background** (*Latar belakang*) | • Problem / Originality (*Masalah / Orisinalitas*)<br>• Foundation & Methodology (*Landasan dan Metodologi*) |
| **AI/ML Innovation Quality** (*Kualitas Inovasi AI/ML*) | • Data and techniques used<br>• Implementation<br>• Model Performance |
| **Innovation Impact** (*Dampak Hasil Inovasi*) | • Relevance to the problem<br>• Level of effectiveness and efficiency produced |

Same flow as Data Story Telling: submission → judge selection → bootcamp & training → presentation before judges → 3 winners (final). Results may be adopted by DJPb.
